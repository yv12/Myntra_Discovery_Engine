## Brand & Style
This design system defines an ultra-crisp, high-density executive intelligence environment tailored for merchandising decision-makers, category managers, and growth strategists. The interface balances high-velocity data density with editorial clarity, channeling the calculated precision of contemporary toolchains like Linear and Stripe Sigma in a pure, clinical light mode.

The visual language rejects decorative clutter in favor of structural clarity, microscopic alignment, and decisive accent signifiers. The aesthetic communicates analytical certainty, frictionless performance, and premium retail telemetry. Key design tenants include:
- **Structural Legibility:** Crisp, razor-thin borders and tiered neutral surfaces replace heavy shadows to demarcate operational planes.
- **Instrument Precision:** Monospaced data tags, tabular telemetry indicators, and strict baseline metrics keep computational analysis clear at any zoom level.
- **Dual-Signaled Semantics:** High-saturation functional accents (Emerald, Electric Teal/Cyan, Amber, Rose) indicate elasticity vectors, actionable levers, and volume shifts against a serene slate foundation.

## Colors
The palette is engineered around high optical contrast, controlled luminance, and zero chromatic glare. Pristine off-whites and cool zinc slates provide the foundation, while focused accent hues serve strict semantic and analytical purposes.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **App Background Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) — The global foundation providing low ocular fatigue under sustained review.
- **Sub-level Shell / Panels:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) — Structural sidebars, dock rails, and analytical tool-drawers.
- **Card & Data Containers:** `#FFFFFF` — Pure white surfaces hosting active charts, telemetry matrices, and data grids.
- **Structural Borders:** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` (hairline grid dividers) and `#E2E8F0` (prominent surface contours).
- **Subtle Surface Hover:** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.

### Typography Neutrals
- **Primary Value:** `#0F172A` (Deep Slate 900) — Headers, key metric figures, and active states.
- **Secondary Slate:** `#475569` (Slate 600) — Table column labels, section descriptions, and metric dimension prefixes.
- **Tertiary Slate:** `#64748B` (Slate 500) — Metadata timestamps, inactive tabs, disabled indicators, and unit symbols.

### Semantic Telemetry & Signal Accents
- **Action & Levers (Electric Cyan / Teal):** `#0284C7` (Primary Base), `#0EA5E9` (Interactive Hover), `#06B6D4` (Focus/Glow). Used for active navigational switches, algorithmic lever handles, and intentional user manipulations.
- **Positive & High Elasticity (Emerald Green):** `#059669` (Base), `#10B981` (Bright highlight), `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.1)` (Background tint). Applied to conversion spikes, wishlist-to-cart velocity, and inventory yield.
- **Warning & Low Sample (Warm Amber):** `#D97706` (Base), `#F59E0B` (Highlight), `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)` (Background tint). Denotes small statistical sample sizes, supply warnings, and split-variant anomalies.
- **Churn & Drop-off (Rose / Coral):** `#E11D48` (Base), `#F43F5E` (Highlight), `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08)` (Background tint). Signifies wishlist abandonment, out-of-stock attrition, and negative conversion variance.

## Typography
The typographic framework integrates three specialized typefaces to balance humanistic brand authority with dense analytical utility:

1. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines & Metric Callouts):** Features crisp, modern geometric architecture with sculpted joins, lending authority and modern tech prestige to page titles, dashboard headings, and high-level summaries.
2. **Inter (Interface & Body Text):** Delivers neutral legibility across tabular datasets, parameter configuration panels, and filter systems.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Metrics, Code, & Telemetry Labels):** Reserved for quantitative numbers, delta percentages, product SKUs, elasticity coefficients, and statistical confidence intervals. Tabular spacing guarantees vertical alignment across multi-row analytical matrices.

### Rules of Application
- Use `headline-xl` exclusively for primary workspace views and executive summaries.
- Apply `label-md` and `label-sm` with uppercase transformation and tracking when rendering analytical dimensions, table column heads, or micro status badges.
- Metric values must always use `JetBrains Mono` at `500` or `600` weight with font-variant-numeric set to `tabular-nums`.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a tight, mathematically consistent 4px/8px modular scale designed for high information density without visual crowding.

### Architectural Layout Grid
- **Desktop Executive Console (>= 1440px):** Fixed navigation rail (256px), dynamic left control filter panel (320px collapsible), and a 12-column fluid data canvas with 16px gutters and 24px outer page margins.
- **Laptop & Standard Desktop (1024px - 1439px):** Collapsible navigation rail (64px icon dock), single or split-column responsive view with 16px gutters and 16px page margins.
- **Tablet / Operational Field Device (768px - 1023px):** Slide-over filters, horizontal scrolling data tables with sticky freeze-panes on SKUs, 8-column layout.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** Single-column stacked stream. Critical KPI banners compress into 2x2 metric matrices; filters collapse into a bottom modal sheet.

### Padding & Breathing Room Philosophy
Internal card padding is calibrated to 16px (`space-base`) or 20px (`space-lg`) to give high-density charts and tables breathing space, preventing visual fatigue across data-heavy dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids dark or diffuse drop-shadows. Depth is articulated through **precise tonal layering**, **low-contrast micro-outlines**, and **directional top-down ambient light**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Floor):** Canvas background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, non-interactive ground.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Shell):** Pristine `#FFFFFF` background bound by a 1px border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Focus / Hover Card):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background with border `#CBD5E1` and an ambient lift of `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays, Flyouts, & Menus):** Pure `#FFFFFF` bound by a 1px border of `#CBD5E1`, with elevation shadow `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
5. **Level 4 (Executive Modals & Drill-downs):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an underlying backdrop scrim of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.3)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

### Structural Borders Over Elevation
Separation of adjacent data regions must rely first on 1px stroke dividers (`#E2E8F0` or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`) rather than shadow-based lift.

## Shapes
A soft geometric aesthetic is used throughout. The interface maintains a restrained corner radius (Level 1) to convey analytical rigor, operational sharpness, and structural integrity.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro UI (Badges, Chips, Segment Controls):** 4px (`0.25rem`).
- **Standard Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** 6px (`0.375rem`).
- **Containers & Surfaces (Cards, Panels, Modals):** 8px (`0.5rem`).
- **Telemetry Indicators (Pills, Dot Signifiers):** 9999px (full circle / pill).

Avoid overly rounded or pill-shaped cards; square and subtle rounded profiles preserve horizontal space, essential for multi-column data visualization and high-density telemetry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Lever / Execute):** Solid `#0284C7` background, `#FFFFFF` bold text, 1px border of `#0369A1`. Hover: `#0EA5E9`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#06B6D4`.
- **Secondary / Utility:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#0F172A` text, 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive / Drop Action:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#E11D48` text, 1px border of `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.2)`. Hover: `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.05)`.
- **Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `#475569` text. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- Built with `JetBrains Mono`, `label-sm` (10px), 4px radius, and horizontal padding of 6px.
- **Positive / High Elasticity:** Background `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`, text `#059669`, border `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`.
- **Lever / Active Filter:** Background `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`, text `#0284C7`, border `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`.
- **Small Sample / Caution:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, text `#D97706`, border `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.
- **Drop / Churn Risk:** Background `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08)`, text `#E11D48`, border `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.2)`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text & Numeric Fields:** `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, 6px radius, height 36px. Text in `Inter` 13px (`#0F172A`), placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a 1px border `#0284C7` and 3px outer glow of `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square or circular bounds, 1px stroke `#CBD5E1`. When selected, filled with `#0284C7` displaying a crisp white micro icon.

### Data Tables & Grid Matrices
- **Table Header:** Height 36px, `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase `label-sm` in `#64748B`, bottom stroke 1px `#E2E8F0`.
- **Table Row:** Height 44px, `#FFFFFF` background, bottom stroke 1px `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Hover state triggers background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Numerical Alignment:** Monospaced numbers must be right-aligned with fixed tabular numeral settings.

### Analytics Cards & Telemetry Panels
- **Structure:** 1px border `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, `#FFFFFF` background, 8px corner radius.
- **Card Header:** Subdued uppercase label (`label-sm`), trailing status badge or 3-dot telemetry menu.
- **Primary Metric Display:** Value rendered in `headline-lg` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), followed by delta badge in `JetBrains Mono` with leading trend arrow (`↑` or `↓`).
